# Cloverwood Logo Family v1.2.1

Status: **APPROVED — LOCKED**

Responsive readability refinement of logo family v1.2. Version v1.2 remains preserved as the previous locked state.

## Approved system

- Display / large: **C3 soft-fade leaf vein**.
- Small / UI: **C2 soft leaf vein + B medium outline/letter thickness**.
- Outer geometry: unchanged.
- Central negative-space cross: unchanged.
- QA: verified on dark and light backgrounds at 72 px, 48 px, and 32 px.

## Readability refinements

- Cloverwood Studio: small variant adjusted for stronger dark-background separation while remaining darker than NUKU.
- Cloverwood: medium outline and `C` thickness in small/UI variant.
- NUKU: medium outline and `N` thickness in small/UI variant.
- Studio OS: organic `S` retained; medium outline and letter thickness in small/UI variant.
- Bridge OS: lighter outline/`B` color plus medium thickness for improved dark-background readability.

## Canonical assets

Each family member has two SVGs:

- `*-display.svg` — C3 display variant.
- `*-small.svg` — C2 + B-thickness small/UI variant.

The three proof PNGs document the final dark, light, and mobile QA views.
